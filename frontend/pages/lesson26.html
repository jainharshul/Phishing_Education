How to Properly Report a Phishing Email

When you find a phishing email in your inbox, your first instinct might be to delete it. However, the most helpful thing you can do is report it properly. Reporting a phishing email helps protect everyone by allowing IT security teams to analyze the threat, block the attacker, and prevent others from falling for the same scam.

Most major email platforms have a built-in "Report Phishing" button. In Gmail, you can click the "Report phishing" link next to the reply button. In Outlook, you can use the "Report Message" feature to classify it as phishing. Using these tools automatically sends a copy of the email to your email provider's security team so they can improve their filters.

You should also forward the suspicious email to your organization's dedicated IT or security mailbox, such as phishing@yourcompany.com or helpdesk@yourcompany.com. When you forward it, do not change the subject line or alter the original email in any way, as this preserves the important technical details in the headers that security experts need to investigate. Never just delete a phishing email; reporting it turns you from a potential victim into an active defender.
